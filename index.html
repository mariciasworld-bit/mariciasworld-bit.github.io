About

Painted with light

I'm a Cairo-based cinematographer, colorist and editor, and the first DOP in Egypt to pioneer infrared cinematography. My work covers music videos, TV series, commercials and live events.

Strong lighting and a full DaVinci Resolve workflow give every project its cinematic feel, from a single reel to a full concert recap.
